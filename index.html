Corporate Profile

Socio-Economic Infrastructure Partner

Established in 2017, **Khutjo Projects 99 (Pty) Ltd** is a 100% black-owned, Level 1 B-BBEE engineering and maintenance services contractor. We specialize in facility upgrades, electrical and plumbing layouts, landscaping installations, and compliant logistics.

As a registered panelist under the **Purchasing Consortium Southern Africa (PURCO SA)**, we serve public academic institutions—including the University of the Witwatersrand (Wits)—delivering routine maintenance and high-pressure infrastructure repairs with guaranteed turnaround times.
